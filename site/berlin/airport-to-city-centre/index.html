Berlin airport to city centre

Prices and journey times checked 2026-11-10. Always confirm with the operator before you travel.

Berlin Brandenburg (BER) is the only airport, south-east of the city and about 25 km from Hauptbahnhof.

How long it takes and how often

The FEX airport express takes about 25 minutes to Hauptbahnhof. S9 and S85 also stop at the airport, every 20 minutes.

Options and prices

  • ABC single ticket to BER: about 5.00 EUR, about 5.20 EUR from 2027
  • Deutschlandticket works on the S-Bahn and regional trains
  • Taxi to the centre: about 55 to 60 EUR
  • Express buses X7, X71 and BER2 (BER2 costs extra)

Where to buy tickets

  • Ticket machines in Terminal 1 on level E0 (baggage claim and bus stops), U1 and the U2 platforms
  • DB travel centre on level U1, about 07:00 to 22:00. Staffed ticket shops on levels E0 and U1
  • Apps: DB Navigator, BVG or VBB Bus & Bahn. Buy a zone ABC ticket
  • FEX, S-Bahn and regional trains: Not possible on board
  • X7 and X71 buses: tickets from the driver, cashless only
  • BER2 bus to Potsdam: ticket only on the bus, with a surcharge
Good to knowBER is in zone C. Use a zone ABC ticket for the airport, not just AB.
