Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Euro Transit Guide is an information site. You don't need an account, and we don't ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details.

What we collect

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers, and the site itself does not set cookies. Our hosting provider, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (for example your IP address, the page requested and the time) to run and secure the service.

Services the site uses

  • Netlify hosts the site and runs the news feature. Cloudflare provides the domain name.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, and jsDelivr may supply the map outlines. Loading them sends your IP address and browser details to those providers.
  • The News tab asks Google News for headlines about the city you opened. The request is made from our server and contains only the city name, not anything about you.
  • Your browser may store copies of pages so the site works offline. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Affiliate links

Some links go to booking partners. If you follow one, that partner may set its own cookies and handle your data under its own policy. See the affiliate disclosure.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we collect almost none, there is usually nothing beyond server logs, which Netlify deletes on its own schedule.
