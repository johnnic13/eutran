Athens airport to city centre

Prices and journey times checked 2026-11-11. Always confirm with the operator before you travel.

Athens International (ATH) is east of the city, about 40 minutes by metro.

How long it takes and how often

Metro line 3 takes about 40 minutes to Syntagma.

Options and prices

  • Metro line 3 / airport ticket: about 9 EUR
  • Express bus X95: about 5.50 EUR
  • Taxi: fixed 50 EUR (day), 65 EUR (night)
  • Suburban rail via Proastiakos

Where to buy tickets

  • Metro line 3 and suburban rail: ticket machines and booths at the airport station. Ticket about 9 EUR.
  • A contactless bank card works at the metro gates, but it is charged the airport fare, about 9 EUR.
  • Express buses X93, X95, X96 and X97: ticket booth outside Arrivals, between exits 4 and 5. About 5.50 EUR.
  • On board the express buses you can tap a contactless bank card. Do not count on buying a paper ticket from the driver.
Good to knowTaxi fares to the centre are fixed. Night fare applies from midnight to 05:00.
