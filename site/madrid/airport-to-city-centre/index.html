Madrid airport to city centre

Prices and journey times checked 2026-11-11. Always confirm with the operator before you travel.

Adolfo Suarez Madrid-Barajas (MAD) is the main airport, on the north-east edge of the city.

How long it takes and how often

Metro line 8 takes about 12 minutes from T1-T2-T3 to Nuevos Ministerios, about 20 minutes from T4. Cercanias C1 also serves terminal T4.

Options and prices

  • Metro: about 1.50 EUR plus 3 EUR airport supplement
  • Express Airport bus: about 5 EUR
  • Taxi to the centre inside the M-30: fixed 33 EUR
  • Cercanias C1 to T4

Where to buy tickets

  • Metro line 8: buy a ticket plus the 3 EUR airport supplement at station machines or ticket offices. Contactless bank cards also work at the gates
  • Cercanias C1 at T4: Renfe machines in the station. Free with many AVE tickets
  • Express Airport bus: about 5 EUR, pay the driver by bank card or use the kiosks at T1, T2, T4 and Atocha. The Tourist Travel Pass is not valid
  • City buses 200 and 101: about 1.50 EUR from the driver, card or cash
  • Free shuttle bus between the terminals, no ticket needed
Good to knowThe fixed taxi fare covers any trip between the airport and the area inside the M-30 ring road, day or night.
